# Rematch progression: contracts and badges

From the game's data assets. Ranked MMR and matchmaking run on the server and are not in the game files.

## Contracts
Each contract has a number of stages. Each stage needs XP, and completing the contract gives a reward amount (unconfirmed currency).

Sorted by XP per stage:

| Contract | Stages | XP per stage | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baller | 3 | 300 | 200 |
| Rookie | 5 | 400 | 300 |
| Amateur | 7 | 500 | 400 |
| Semi-pro | 10 | 750 | 500 |
| Wonder | 15 | 1000 | 600 |
| Pro | 20 | 1200 | 700 |
| Star | 30 | 1400 | 800 |
| Champion | 40 | 1600 | 900 |
| Hero | 50 | 1800 | 1000 |
| Icon | **100** | 2000 | 1100 |

## Badges
There are badge categories for Attack, Defense, Dribble, Movement, Playmaking, Goalkeeper, Clutch, Performance, General and Random.

Every badge checked so far has 4 levels:

| Level | Progress needed |
|---|---|
| 1 | 25 |
| 2 | 125 |
| 3 | 500 |
| 4 | 1250 |

Goalkeeper badges: Aggressive Keeper, Back To Front, Bump, Dauntless, Doorman, I'll Do It Myself, Save, Sweeper, The Wall, Throwback.
